Best choices

What to pick, and why, worked out from the game's own numbers.

How stats combine

The single most useful rule: damage bonuses in the same bracket add, different brackets multiply. So spreading bonuses across brackets beats stacking one.

  • Damage brackets (community-documented): Damage Tome; flat damage items (Gym Sauce, Brass Knuckles, Idle Juice); the main % bracket (Boss Buster, Tactical Glasses, Beefy Ring, Gamer Goggles and others); elite damage; and a few solo brackets (Speed Boi, Corrupted Sword, Noelle). Example: +50% in two brackets is 1.5 × 1.5 = 2.25×; +100% in one bracket is only 2.0×.
  • The Damage Tome is its own bracket, worth ×(1 + 0.08 per level) at Common. Fifty levels is ×5 on every weapon.
  • Crits apply after the brackets. Giant Fork Megacrits are 4× on top (game data).
  • Proc chance can pass 100%. 120% poison chance means one guaranteed stack plus a 20% chance of a second, so on-hit items keep scaling with copies.
  • Each weapon has a proc coefficient that multiplies item trigger chances (game data): Sniper Rifle 1.5; Dice, Revolver, Bow, Bone, Shotgun, Lightning Staff, Firestaff and most single shots 1.0; Katana 0.95; Axe, Aura, Black Hole and Dragon's Breath 0.7. Overpowered Lamp adds an extra independent roll: 40% becomes 64%.
  • Evasion has diminishing returns and tops out around 60% (community figure); the screen shows the raw total. More than about three Slippery Rings is wasted.
  • Offer rarity multiplies every tome and weapon pick by 1.0–2.0× (game data), and Luck decides the rarity. That makes Luck a multiplier on all your other growth.

Tomes: which four

For a high-kill run the four slots should cover leveling, offer quality, enemy count and damage. Your recorded runs used Luck, XP, Cursed and Chaos; Chaos is the weakest of those for this goal.

Pick Tome Per level (Common) Why
1 XP +7% XP More levels means more weapon and tome picks, and for Dicehead one extra Gamba roll per level.
2 Luck +8% Luck Raises the rarity of every offer (×1.2–2.0 per pick) and of items, so it multiplies all your other growth.
3 Cursed +3.5% Difficulty (4–7% after rounding) More enemies, so more kills and XP. Difficulty starts near 0%, and the stage-1 swarm needs about 500% for full spawns.
4 Quantity or Damage +1 projectile, or ×1.08 in its own bracket Dice and Katana start with 1 projectile, so the first Quantity levels double and triple their output. Damage is the steadier late-game pick.
Swap in Precision +7% Crit For crit builds. Dice and Katana already get crit from their own upgrade lines.
Swap in Shield +25 Shield If you die to chip damage in swarms.

How to reach 500% Difficulty: your two Cursed Tome runs reached 54%. That came from about 6 Cursed levels plus 4 Greed Shrines (+5% each). For 500% you'd need roughly 100 Common Cursed levels, or fewer at higher rarity, plus all 8 Greed Shrines on each map (+40%), Skuleg (+7% a copy) and Cursed Grabbies (+10% a copy). Sir Chadwell gets +1% Difficulty per character level for free (see Characters below).

Weapons: what makes one scale

Three numbers decide how far a weapon grows: how fast it attacks, how much each Damage line adds compared with its base, and whether its upgrades include crit lines (crits multiply after every damage bracket). The proc coefficient decides how well it triggers on-hit items.

Revolver 1.4 s +2.5 on 5 (+50%) Crit +10%, Crit Damage +20% 1.0 6 projectiles plus bounce lines; strong crit and proc carrier

For Dicehead: Dice plus Katana, the pairing in three of the four top runs. Revolver or Bone are the best third weapons if you want more crit carriers; Sniper Rifle if you're stacking on-hit items. megabonk.org warns that more weapons spread your upgrades thinner, and the top Dicehead runs mostly used two.

Items: what to take and what to banish

Ranked for high-kill runs, using the game's numbers.

Tier Items Why
Always take Wizard's Hat, Pot, Anvil Raise tome and weapon level caps, or add a stat line to every weapon pick. Anvil is max 1, so take it whenever it appears.
Always take Cursed Doll Each copy makes 2 enemies lose 30% of their Max HP a second. It ignores how tough enemies get at high difficulty, and it's Common and cheap (about 28 gold in shops).
Always take Clover, Time Bracelet, Echo Shard Luck +10% and XP +8% a copy, plus extra XP shards. Cheap engine items.
Strong Giant Fork +15% crit and 14% Megacrits at 4×, a copy. The biggest crit multiplier.
Strong Joe's Dagger 1% execute; each execute adds +1% Damage, with no cap on stacks. Max 10 copies.
Strong Chonkplate, Holy Book Lifesteal and overheal (+75% and +25% a copy); Holy Book turns healing into damage. This is how the top runs ended with 40,000–150,000 shield on about 200 HP.
Strong Idle Juice + Campfire Standing still adds +4% damage a second (to +100% a copy) and heals 1,100 HP a minute. Both reward the ledge.
Strong Dragonfire, Lightning Orb, Ice Cube, Overpowered Lamp Chance-based procs that keep scaling past 100%; Lamp adds an extra roll (max 2). Best on 1.0+ proc weapons.
Strong Gym Sauce, Beefy Ring, Boss Buster Sit in different damage brackets, so they multiply each other.
Situational Kevin Keeps on-damage effects firing and can't kill you. Banish once Bob's Light can't drop anymore (the 1.1M run's rule).
Situational Skuleg, Cursed Grabbies, Old Mask More difficulty or elites, so more kills, if your build can take it.
Banish Spiky Shield, Scarf, Eagle Claw, Feathers Airborne or armor items that don't help a ledge farm.
Banish Golden Glove, Golden Sneakers, Golden Shield Gold stops mattering once chests and shops are done.
Banish Slurp Gloves, Mirror (after stage 1) Small heals and cooldown effects that fall off.
Banish late Slippery Ring after about 3 Evasion has diminishing returns, topping out around 60%.

With the Toggler, turning off the banish rows means every chest and shop roll lands on the tiers above.

Characters: whose passive solves what

Passives grow every level, and long runs reach very high levels: your 62-minute run hit 1,043, and the 1.1M run reached 3,118. So a small per-level number becomes the biggest stat in the run.

megabonk.org found Noelle and Fox most often among 1,223 runs over 300,000 kills. The four top Dicehead runs show he can go further, to 700k–1.1M.

If difficulty is your bottleneck, which it was in your last two runs, Sir Chadwell is worth a try. His passive does the Cursed Tome's job automatically, and you unlock him by clearing Forest Tier 3.
