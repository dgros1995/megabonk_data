Item database

All 87 items in v1.0.69, with the exact numbers the game uses, read from its own item database. "Per copy" values add up with each extra copy unless a limit is shown. 85 can drop; Golden Ring is not in the drop pool, and Cage Key and Crypt Key are quest items, left out below.

Role marks what an item does for a high-kill run: Kill (damage that keeps up at high difficulty), Survive, Scale (levels, XP, luck), Economy, Difficulty (raises it, which means more enemies), or Situational. ★ = on the 1.1M run's toggle list.

Notes

  • Rarities above are the game's own. Bob (Dead), Beefy Ring and Credit Card (Green) are Epic; megabonk.org lists them as Common.
  • Damage that scales with enemy health (Cursed Doll, Joe's Dagger, Bob's Light) keeps working at high difficulty, where flat damage falls behind.
  • Old Mask on the toggle list is likely there because more elites means more kills and drops, the same logic as difficulty.
  • A few community figures differ from the game data: Gas Mask's caps, Holy Book's overheal and Big Bonk's per-copy growth weren't published anywhere.

Sources: Bloxodes item database, megabonk.org item database, rockmanzexo 1.1M run.
