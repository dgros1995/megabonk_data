Weapon guide

Weapons attack on their own and grow through weapon upgrade offers. Published per-weapon numbers are sparse, so this tab pairs the community list with what was measured in game.

How weapons scale

  • Each weapon upgrade raises a few stat lines of that weapon, chosen by the offer. A level-41 Katana offer in the 1.1M run read: Size 868% → 888%, Crit Chance 288% → 296%, Projectile Count 31.6 → 32.6.
  • Anvil adds one more stat line to future weapon offers, so each pick gives more.
  • Your tomes and items apply on top. A weapon's own stats multiply with your Damage, Crit, Size, Attack Speed and so on, which is why the weapon's "scales with" column matters when you pick tomes.
  • The level cap is 40, plus about 10 for each Pot (less for each extra copy). Rizzotto's 4-weapon run ended with all four at 40; the recorded run with one Pot capped at 50; the 1.1M run reached 111 and 112.
  • Slots: 4 weapons. megabonk.org's Dicehead guide warns that more weapons spread upgrades thinner; the top Dicehead runs used 2.

Damage lines are flat additions to the weapon's base, so a weapon with low base damage (Dice, Black Hole, Poison Flask) gains the most from each Damage line relative to where it started. Dice and Katana both carry two crit lines, which is why they pair well with Precision and Giant Fork. Dice's own crit growth from rolling sixes is capped by the game at 100 roll-ups a minute.
