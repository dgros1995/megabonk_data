Weapons

Weapons attack on their own and grow through weapon upgrade offers. The exact numbers below come from the game's weapon database.

How weapons scale

  • Each weapon upgrade raises a few stat lines of that weapon, chosen by the offer, by the Common amount × the offer's rarity multiplier (×1.0 Common up to ×2.0 Legendary).
  • Anvil adds one more stat line to future weapon offers.
  • Your tomes and items apply on top. The weapon's damage is multiplied by your Damage stat (all three brackets), and its crit chance adds to yours.
  • The level cap is 40, plus 10 for the first Pot and 1 less for each extra copy (never below +2).
  • Slots: 4 weapons.

Proc coefficients

On-hit item chances are multiplied by the weapon's proc coefficient: Sniper Rifle 1.5; Dice, Revolver, Bow, Bone, Shotgun, Lightning Staff, Firestaff and most single shots 1.0; Katana 0.95; Axe, Aura, Black Hole, Dragon's Breath 0.7.

Damage lines are flat additions to the weapon's base, so a weapon with low base damage (Dice, Black Hole, Poison Flask) gains the most from each Damage line relative to where it started. Dice, Katana, Revolver, Shotgun, Bow and Bone carry crit lines, and since crit past 100% overcrits (2 crits ×4, 3 crits ×6.25), those lines scale harder than they look. Dice's own crit growth from rolling sixes is capped by the game at 100 roll-ups a minute.

How each tome changes each weapon

All values are from the weapon data and the code that reads them (WeaponUtility.GetDuration, GetAttackSizeMultiplier, GetProjectileSpeed, GetAttackQuantity, GetWeaponCooldown, GetDamageContainer). The formulas:

  • Damage: every hit × your Damage stat.
  • Precision: your crit chance + the weapon's own.
  • Cooldown (Attack Speed): pause = weapon cooldown ÷ Attack Speed; shots inside a burst are spaced burst time ÷ projectiles ÷ Attack Speed.
  • Quantity: weapon projectiles + yours.
  • Size: weapon size × yours, capped where the weapon has a cap.
  • Duration: weapon duration × yours, capped at the weapon's max duration.
  • Projectile Speed: weapon speed × yours; only matters if something travels.
  • Knockback: weapon knockback × yours, applied on every hit.
